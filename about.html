About Temple Yatra

Namaste! I am Pradeep from Telangana.

I made Temple Yatra because I love visiting temples. I wanted one place where everyone can find temple timings, history, and how to reach - in simple language.

Our site has information about 241 temples across India - Tirupati, Srisailam, Yadagirigutta, Varanasi and many more.

Our mission is simple: Help every devotee plan their yatra easily.
